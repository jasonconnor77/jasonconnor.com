Systems & tools

I am not trying to replace the ERP. I am trying to make its information easier to use.

Most ERP systems contain the information the operation needs. The challenge is getting the right answer fast enough to act on it.

Google SheetsApps ScriptExcelERP data

01 / Production control

Make the next decision obvious.

Production tools should answer the questions that decide whether the day goes right: what should run next, what is late, what is currently running, where labor is deployed, where capacity is available, and which jobs are at risk.

Production-priority views
Real-time floor status
Scheduling & capacity views

02 / Material visibility

See the shortage before the machine does.

Material shortages are easier to solve before a job reaches the machine. The goal is to surface availability, open requirements, commitments, replenishment, and risk early enough to change the outcome.

Open material requirements
Inventory commitments

03 / Management views

Manage by exception, not by scrolling.

I build reporting around the things that need attention rather than forcing managers to review everything. Good management information should narrow the field, not create another report to maintain.

04 / Technology

The manufacturing question comes first.

Google Sheets, Google Apps Script, Microsoft Excel, ERP data, automated reporting, dashboards, and workflow tools are means to an end. The objective is to understand the problem first, then build the simplest useful way to answer it.

Manufacturing questionWhat decision must be made?
Useful informationWhat data actually matters?
Useful tools answer real operating questions.If a tool adds work, hides the answer, or takes too much explanation, it will not help the operation.
