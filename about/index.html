About

I’ve built my career around turning data into plans that work.

Forecasting, material requirements, master scheduling, inventory analysis, purchasing, and production experience help me connect the numbers to the work that needs to happen.

Connecting Data · People · Priorities

Good planning begins with understanding what the data is telling you.

I enjoy working with data: spotting patterns, building forecasts, evaluating material requirements, identifying constraints, and creating tools that make complex operations easier to understand. Over more than 20 years, I have worked across production planning, master scheduling, materials, supplier performance, inventory, and operations.

At MAC Valves, I helped establish the Taiwan Division’s reporting, production scheduling, and warehouse systems from the ground up. I developed four-month material requirements plans using forecasts and demand, coordinated purchasing timing, and supported growth from 5 to more than 1,000 SKUs.

At Axis Engineering, I managed integrated schedules for about 50 concurrent tooling projects across Engineering, machining, outside suppliers, and assembly. I combined Excel schedules, ERP information, and department updates to track milestones while working with Purchasing and suppliers to protect deadlines.

My approach is to understand demand, check materials and capacity, make the risks visible, create a workable schedule, and measure the outcome. I value clear communication and plans grounded in actual operating conditions.

My operations leadership experience strengthens the analytical side of my work. Having managed production and materials teams, I know what happens after a schedule is released and how staffing, machine availability, supplier performance, and real shop-floor conditions affect execution.

I am comfortable with complex requirements, changing forecasts, past-due orders, shortages, competing priorities, and schedules that need to be rebuilt. I enjoy finding the underlying issue and improving the process rather than repeatedly reacting to the symptom.

I have built planning and reporting systems from scratch at MAC Valves, Axis Engineering, and Bunting. Using Excel, Google Sheets, Apps Script, and ERP data, I automate KPI calculations, highlight exceptions, evaluate supplier quotations, and give teams clearer information for planning and scheduling decisions.
