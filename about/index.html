About

I build the plan, lead the work, and measure the result.

My experience spans planning and leading the people who execute it. I connect forecasts, inventory, suppliers, labor, and capacity—and I build tools that make the work easier to direct and improve.

Leadership · Planning · Scheduling · Execution

A schedule is only as strong as the demand, material, and capacity decisions behind it.

I’m most engaged when there is a complex problem to solve. Demand changes. Suppliers miss dates. Inventory looks available until the next order is released. Machines and people have real limits. I like finding the patterns, determining what is holding the operation back, and giving teams clear, workable priorities.

At Bunting Bearings, I returned to a serious scheduling challenge and took direct control of the production schedule during recovery. Today I lead 30+ associates and two supervisors in an environment with 350+ shop orders. I stabilized a standard product line spanning more than 1,400 SKUs and reduced past-due order lines from 2,432 to 30 in under 24 months. Earlier, as Production Scheduler, I helped increase output 40% and clear a backlog of 350 past-due orders without adding headcount.

At Axis Engineering, I managed integrated schedules for 50+ concurrent tooling projects—from Engineering and machining through outside suppliers and final assembly—and improved supplier on-time delivery from 75% to 95%. As Materials Manager, I led a nine-person team, set inventory min/max levels and reorder points, improved material flow, and built an RFQ process to compare supplier pricing and lead times. I know how closely leadership, suppliers, and materials decisions affect the schedule.

I don’t believe a schedule is successful just because it looks good on a screen. It has to reflect real inventory, supplier commitments, machine capacity, labor, and customer priorities—and it has to change when those conditions change.

At MAC Valves, I helped build the Taiwan Division’s planning, reporting, and warehouse systems from the ground up. I planned complete product models and BOM-based material requirements four months ahead, coordinating purchasing timing and supplier needs while supporting growth from 5 to more than 1,000 SKUs. I also managed more than $10 million in inventory while maintaining 99.2% accuracy.

I build the tools I wish existed when I need better answers. Across all three companies, I have developed Excel, Google Sheets, and Apps Script systems from scratch for scheduling, materials, inventory, supplier quotations, and KPI reporting. At Bunting, those tools automatically calculate performance metrics and flag operational problems, helping my supervisors and me spend less time preparing reports and more time acting on them.

What distinguishes my experience is that I’ve worked on both sides of the plan: making it and being accountable for the people, materials, and results behind it. I bring that perspective to management, planning, scheduling, supplier relationships, and process improvement.
