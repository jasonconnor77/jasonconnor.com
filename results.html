How the results were built

Better visibility changes the quality of decisions.

The improvements came from operating discipline: clearer priorities, better capacity use, stronger supplier follow-up, more visible material risk, and practical systems that make exceptions easier to see.

01

Make the problem visible

Separate the urgent from the noisy. Surface late work, shortages, capacity constraints, and execution gaps in a form managers can use.

02

Establish a credible priority

Translate demand, due dates, constraints, material, and capacity into a shop-floor sequence that reflects actual conditions.

03

Close the execution loop

Follow through on commitments, adjust when conditions change, and measure whether the corrective action actually improved the result.
