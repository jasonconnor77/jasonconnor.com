Systems & tools

I build tools that help people plan, lead, and act.

I build rolling schedules and performance tools that consider customer demand, due dates, machine capacity, labor availability, material readiness, changeovers, and production throughput.

04 / Automation

Automate the process. Improve the decision.

I built custom Excel planning systems at MAC Valves, Axis Engineering, and Bunting. At Axis, I also developed an RFQ workflow using imported engineering BOMs to send targeted requests to suppliers suited to the work, capture quotes, compare pricing and lead times, and recommend options for my final review.
